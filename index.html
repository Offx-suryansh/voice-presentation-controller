About this project

The application is designed to render supported presentation files in the browser, provide voice and keyboard navigation, and keep presentation history in local browser storage. Presentation files are processed locally by the app; voice recognition behaviour can depend on your browser provider.

Privacy

PDF and PowerPoint files are read and rendered in your browser; this application does not upload presentation files or audio recordings.
Voice recognition uses your browser's speech recognition capability; depending on the browser, it may be processed by the browser vendor's service.
PowerPoint font substitutions may be requested from Google Fonts when needed.
